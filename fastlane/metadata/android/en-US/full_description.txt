<p><i>Android Units</i> is a powerful unit-aware calculator that can also easily perform simple unit conversions. It's inspired by GNU Units.</p><p>This edition is a continuation of <a href='https://github.com/xxv/Units' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>Steve Pomeroy's original app</a>.</p><p><br><b>Usage</b></p><p>Anything that is shown within a dotted box can be entered into Units. Eg. <code>m³÷hr</code>.</p><p>Place the value and the unit you want to convert from in the "you have" box (eg. <code>4.9inches</code>) and the unit you wish to convert to in the "you want" box (eg. <code>cm</code>). To enter units, either press the "unit" button and select from the list, or tap on the input box a second time and an on-screen keyboard should pop up.</p><p>You can enter simple units, such as <code>cm</code> / <code>centimeter</code> or complex units, such as <code>m^3÷hr</code>. You can press the "unit" button to show a list of all the units. If you don't specify a "to" unit, it will provide a definition in base units. For a list of examples, please see below.</p><p>More hints can be found in the project's Readme.</p>
